01 SPECTROSCOPY · MATERIALS Solid-state NMR & battery materials Probing local chemical environments and structural changes in electrochemical materials. +

Research overview

Solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance (ssNMR) provides a local probe of chemical environments in complex solids. This research area focuses on using NMR spectroscopy to investigate battery materials, local structure, and changes associated with electrochemical processes.

Research themes

  • Local atomic structure and chemical environments
  • NMR characterization of battery materials
  • Structural changes during electrochemical processes
